**Grammar & Style Analysis**

* **Parallel Structure in Lists:** Notice how each bullet point begins with an imperative verb (**Automate**, **Visualize**, **Prompt**) followed by a direct object. Maintaining strict parallel structure keeps action-oriented copy crisp and easy for readers to scan on a back cover.
* **Active vs. Passive Voice:** Phrases like "build lightweight data pipelines" and "eliminate repetitive manual reporting" place the owner in the driver's seat. Passive constructions (e.g., "repetitive manual reporting can be eliminated") weaken sales copy and should be avoided here.
* **Targeted Vocabulary:** Terms like *plumbing*, *framework*, and *blueprint* frame technical tasks (pipelines and dashboard architecture) as structured, manageable projects rather than intimidating technical hurdles.
