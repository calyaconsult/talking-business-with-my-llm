# Title and Subtitle Ideas

**Talking Business with My LLM** is a solid, engaging title, provided you capitalize the possessive pronoun **"My"** to align with standard American English title case rules.

**Strengths**

* **Engaging Contrast:** Pairing "talking business" (a classic idiom rooted in human commerce) with "LLM" creates a clever, modern juxtaposition.
* **Clear Approach:** It immediately signals a practical, conversational guide to leveraging technology rather than a dense technical textbook.

**Weaknesses & Refinements**

* **Jargon vs. Broad Appeal:** While **LLM** (Large Language Model) is standard terminology in tech circles, general business readers and C-suite executives often respond more immediately to **AI**. If your book is tailored to technical managers or prompt engineers, "LLM" works great. If you are targeting a broader corporate audience, "AI" might catch more eyes on a shelf.
* **Missing Value Proposition:** Main titles in business publishing usually establish the concept, but a subtitle is needed to sell the outcome. Readers want to know what profit, efficiency, or strategic advantage they gain by picking up the book.

**How to Elevate It**
Keep your title as the punchy hook, but anchor it with a results-oriented subtitle:

* *Talking Business with My LLM: How to Prompt Strategy, Automate Workflows, and Drive Growth*
* *Talking Business with My AI: An Executive's Guide to Co-Pilot Leadership*

## Ideas for a Subtitle

**Action & Efficiency Focus (Best for showing instant ROI)**

* *A Quick-Read Playbook for Boosting Productivity with AI*
* *Practical Strategies to Save Time, Automate Workflows, and Scale*
* *The 20-Minute Guide to Turning Generative AI into Real ROI*

**Executive & Leadership Focus (Best for reaching decision-makers)**

* *A Practical Guide to Prompting Strategy and Driving Business Growth*
* *How to Delegate, Automate, and Scale Using AI*
* *Bridging the Gap Between AI Tech and Executive Strategy*

**Client Magnet Focus (Best for positioning yourself as an expert)**

* *A Concise Blueprint for Leaders Ready to Leverage AI*
* *Simple Prompts and Proven Frameworks for the Modern Business Owner*
* *The Fast-Track Guide to Integrating AI into Your Daily Operations*

## More Ideas

**Data & Automation Subtitle Options**

* *Talking Business with My LLM: A Small Business Guide to Automating Data, Dashboards, and Decisions*
* *Talking Business with My AI: How Small Business Owners Turn Raw Data into Everyday Action*
* *Talking Business with My LLM: The 20-Minute Blueprint for Pipelines, Dashboards, and Smarter Growth*

**Style & Positioning Notes**

* **Bridging the Tech Gap:** Small business owners often suffer from scattered spreadsheets and missing metrics. Pairing an accessible title (*Talking Business*) with concrete subtitle terms (*Dashboards*, *Data*, *Pipelines*) immediately tells the reader that you solve both high-level strategy and the underlying technical plumbing.
* **Active Verbs:** Using action-oriented phrasing like *Automating Data*, *Turning Raw Data into Action*, and *Smarter Growth* promises clear financial and operational outcomes rather than abstract tech concepts.
